Usage Guidelines

Keep it recognizable.

The logo works hard for us. A few simple rules keep it consistent everywhere it appears.

Do

  • Use the light-mode mark on light backgrounds and the dark-mode mark on dark ones
  • Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the height of the mark
  • Scale the logo proportionally in both directions
  • Use the SVG file whenever the platform supports it, for crisp rendering at any size

Don't

  • Recolor, recreate, or apply effects (drop shadows, outlines, gradients) to the logo
  • Stretch, squash, or rotate the logo
  • Place the light-mode mark on a light background or vice versa
  • Crowd the logo with other text, logos, or graphics
