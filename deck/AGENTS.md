# AGENTS GUIDE

## Overview
This document serves as a guide for agents to effectively use the sales pitch deck to promote our services.

## Purpose of the Sales Pitch Deck
The sales pitch deck, accessible at [Sales Pitch Deck](https://conferencecaptioning.com/deck/index.html), is designed to:
- Highlight the key features of our services.
- Showcase the benefits to potential clients.
- Provide a professional and engaging presentation.

## How to Use the Sales Pitch Deck
1. Open the pitch deck in a browser using the provided link.
2. Familiarize yourself with the content and flow of the presentation.
3. Use the deck during client meetings or presentations to explain our services.

## Key Points to Emphasize
- **Accessibility**: Highlight how our services improve accessibility for all users.
- **Reliability**: Emphasize the dependability of our solutions.
- **Customization**: Showcase how our services can be tailored to meet client needs.
