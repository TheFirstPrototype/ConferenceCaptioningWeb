Why It's Still Unsolved

The two existing options both break at the event.

Human captioners

Accurate, but doesn't scale

  • ✕Costly, and booked weeks ahead
  • ✕One person, one language
  • ✕No backup if they drop or get sick
Cloud AI

Fast, but fragile

  • ✕Needs a dedicated internet line: $2,500–$5,000 per event
  • ✕Ballroom Wi-Fi drops, so captions drop
  • ✕Audio leaves the building, which many organizers can't allow

Result: most events skip it, or deliver one language to one audience.
