Hook. Michigan's own state survey counts about 733,000 deaf or hard-of-hearing residents, and over a million people speak another language at home. Live events either pay for a human captioner or have no captions at all.
Problem. Human captioning is expensive, booked weeks ahead, and can't translate. Michigan's Arabic, Spanish and Asian communities can barely follow a live event in their language.
Solution. Scan a QR code and you're in. Live captions and translation in 70+ languages, no app, no hardware. OBS, Zoom, venue screens, a shareable transcript link.
Proof. Open benchmark on a live NASA press conference, on-device: 97.2% word accuracy, 0.05 s delay, failover in as little as half a second. Methodology, data and scorer are public.
Why Michigan. Universities, auto and manufacturing, one of the largest Arab-American communities, and ADA obligations across schools, churches and municipalities.
Ask. We have captioned and translated in 6+ countries and 20+ cities, over a million words. Because the AI runs on the organizer's own device, it works offline, so events skip the $2,500 to $5,000 dedicated internet line. Raising $1.25M: 45% sales, 35% product, 20% operations and compliance. We have $30K ARR, with YMCA and Apple among our customers. Pricing: $150/hr for events, $400/month unlimited on an annual commitment for for-profits, with non-profit discounts. Start with a pilot in your network.
