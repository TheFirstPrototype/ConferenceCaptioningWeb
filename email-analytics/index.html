How accurate are opens? An open is counted when the email's tiny tracking picture is loaded. Gmail, Yahoo and Apple Mail often load it for the reader in advance, and some readers block pictures, so opens are an estimate. "Opened by a person" leaves out the opens we can recognise as a mail provider's image proxy (marked * below), but Apple Mail's privacy feature cannot be told apart. Clicks are reliable.
