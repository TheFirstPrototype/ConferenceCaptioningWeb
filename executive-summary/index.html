Enterprise Reliability

When the internet goes down, the captions don't have to.

Production teams cannot gamble on venue internet stability. Because ConferenceCaptioning runs neural models locally, your event remains completely protected from outside connectivity drops.

Multi-System Automatic Failover

A primary and backup Mac operate on the same local network. If the primary host fails or drops audio, the backup system automatically assumes real-time output, complete with configurable retry intervals and alerts.
