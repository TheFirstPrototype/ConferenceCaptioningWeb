What you earn

Cash partners earn commission on every referred customer. The more customers you sign in a year, the higher your rate. Non-profits can choose account credits or a donation instead.

TierSigned customers in the last 12 monthsCommission
Silver1–215%
Gold3–520%
Platinum6 or moreNegotiable with scale

Your tier rate is a percentage of what the customer actually paid for their first event or first year, after any discount. After that, you earn 10% of what the customer pays for the next 24 months, counted from their first payment. Platinum is negotiable with scale: we agree your rate together.

The Standard plan is $400 per month billed annually, or $4,800 a year. A customer you refer gets 10% off their first year, so they pay $4,320. Your commission is a percentage of what they actually paid.

What your referral gets

Customers you refer get 10% off their first event or first year. Non-profits are eligible for our existing non-profit discount of up to 50% off their first event or first year, and a partner referral makes the full discount more likely.

How credit works

  • Attribution window: 180 days. A customer who creates a new account within 180 days of clicking your link is credited to you.
  • Most recent link wins. If a customer clicked links from two partners, the more recent one gets the credit.
  • New customers only. Existing accounts don’t qualify.
  • Or use your code. The customer can enter your partner code when they sign up or mention it on a demo call.
  • Manual approval and payouts. We review every application. Payouts are made by us after the customer has paid.

Built on purpose

Every part of the program is a choice, made for how events actually get bought.

  • Credit that lasts 180 days. Events are booked months in advance, so a short tracking window would miss the introduction you made.
  • Credit on a demo call. Many of our customers start with a conversation, not a checkout. Have them say your code on the call and you are credited even if they never click your link.
  • Commission that continues. After the first event or year, you keep earning 10% of what the customer pays for the next 24 months.
  • Both sides win. The people you refer get a discount, so recommending us is an easy yes.
  • Non-profits are first-class partners. Credits or a donation to your organization, for people who cannot take a commission.
  • A person on the other end. We review every application and make every payout ourselves, so you work with us, not a ticket queue.
  • See what is happening. Your dashboard lists every account you referred: who signed up, whether the account is still active, whether it became a paying customer, and what you earned.

Partner rules

Tell people you earn a commission when you recommend us. Use only our published accuracy and delay numbers (see the benchmark) and don’t make other claims about what the product does. Referring yourself doesn’t earn a commission.

You can see the name and email of each account created through your link or code, and whether it became a paying customer. Use that information only to support the customer, and don’t share it or use it for anything else.

Cash payouts may require tax information, for example a W-9 for partners in the United States. We tell you what we need when you are approved.

Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid?

After the referred customer has signed and paid for their first event or first year, and after each later payment that earns the ongoing 10%. Payouts are manual and we agree the method when you are approved.

How do you know a customer came from me?

Your link carries your partner code. We store it for 180 days and attach it to the new account when the customer signs up. A customer can also enter your code at sign-up or mention it on a demo call.

Can I refer my own company?

No. Referring yourself doesn’t earn a commission.

What counts as a new customer?

An account created after someone clicked your link (within 180 days), or one that gave your code at sign-up or on a demo call. Existing accounts don’t qualify.

Can non-profits take cash?

Non-profit partners choose account credits or a donation to their organization instead of cash.

How are the tiers counted?

By the number of customers you signed in the last 12 months: Silver is 1 to 2, Gold is 3 to 5, and Platinum is 6 or more. Platinum is negotiable with scale, so we agree your rate together.

What if two partners referred the same customer?

The more recent link gets the credit.

What can I see about my referrals?

Your dashboard lists every account you referred: who signed up, their stream, whether the account is still active, whether it became a paying customer, and what you earned.
