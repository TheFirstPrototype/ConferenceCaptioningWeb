Please don’t say

  • Any number that is not on the list above, or words like “100% accurate”, “instant” or “no delay”.
  • That attendees’ phones work with no network at all. Phones reach the captions over the event’s local network or mobile data.
  • Comparisons with other companies, or other companies’ prices and numbers.
  • Customer names, logos, quotes or stories that are not already on our website. Don’t invent any.
  • Prices other than the ones we publish, or words like “guaranteed”, “certified” or “patented”.
  • Anything that hides the fact that you earn a commission.
